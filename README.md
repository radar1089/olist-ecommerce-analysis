# 📊 Olist E-Commerce Business Analysis

An end-to-end Data Analytics project using the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset. This project aims to analyze business performance, identify revenue trends, understand customer behavior, evaluate seller performance, and investigate the root cause of the revenue decline in June 2017.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project covers the complete data analytics workflow:

- Data Cleaning
- Data Profiling
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Business Analysis
- Dashboard Development in Power BI
- Business Recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights

- Revenue consistently increased throughout 2017 before experiencing a significant decline in June.
- A small number of product categories contributed a large portion of total revenue.
- Customer satisfaction decreased as delivery delays increased.
- Credit Card was the dominant payment method.
- Revenue decline in June 2017 was associated with a decrease in active sellers, fewer customer orders, and lower sales in several major product categories.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

- Improve logistics performance to reduce delivery delays.
- Maintain active sellers through retention programs.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing product categories.
- Monitor seller performance continuously.
- Enhance customer experience to improve review ratings.
